## Supersession notice

The owner has withdrawn the former global art-direction constraints and requested a reset of the way RetailOS experiences are explored and approved.

The following former direction is no longer active:
- Burgundy/dark-red as a mandatory dominant identity.
- A fixed Vietnamese-first typography direction as a visual-system mandate (Vietnamese content and correct glyph rendering remain functional requirements where applicable).
- Authentic retail photography as a required visual treatment.
- The previous supplied-image/video role mapping as a standing global art-direction rule.
- Any prescribed component, material, card, composition, motion or style preference inherited from this M0 specification.

No replacement aesthetic has been selected. Do not silently substitute minimal, Liquid Glass or another named style as the new default.

## What remains useful as historical evidence

- The M0 work identified important product surfaces and workflows: Login, Shell/navigation, Home, Module Library, Stocktake, Stocktake Import, Settings/Update Center, Search/Notifications/Activity, Scanner presentation and system states.
- Existing source architecture audits, CSS inventories, responsive checks and motion/performance evidence remain useful as observations of the source at their recorded commit.
- Technical invariants and domain contracts remain active through their owning documents, not through this historical visual specification.
- Browser emulation, CI, physical-device acceptance and owner approval remain distinct evidence/decision categories.

## How to continue

For new or redesigned experiences, follow the discovery, creative exploration, prototype, feasibility, explicit owner approval, implementation and QA workflow in RETAILOS_PRODUCT_SKILL.md. Revalidate every inherited audit finding against current source before acting.

Do not delete this historical document or its linked audit evidence as part of the reset. Its presence is not an instruction to continue M0 under the old visual direction.
